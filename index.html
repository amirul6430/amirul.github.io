About Me

I am an interdisciplinary researcher working across the social sciences and Asian studies. My academic interests include marriage and family studies, child marriage, forced marriage, gender-based violence, digital violence, cultural studies, bibliometric analysis, and contemporary social issues.


My research employs quantitative, qualitative, bibliometric, spatial, and mixed-method approaches to investigate complex social and cultural phenomena. I am particularly interested in connecting empirical research from Bangladesh and South Asia with broader international scholarship.


I am currently pursuing advanced academic studies in Japanese and Asian Studies and developing interdisciplinary research at the intersection of society, culture, language, and contemporary social issues.
