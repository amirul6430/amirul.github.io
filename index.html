About Me

I am an interdisciplinary researcher with research interests spanning social sciences, anthropology, sociology, cultural studies, marriage and family studies, and Asian studies. My research combines quantitative, qualitative, bibliometric, and spatial approaches to investigate contemporary social and cultural issues.


I am currently pursuing advanced academic studies in Japanese and Asian Studies and developing research projects that connect South Asian realities with broader interdisciplinary scholarship.
